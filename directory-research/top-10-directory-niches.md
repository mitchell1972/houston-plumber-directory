## 1. Therapist / Mental Health Professional Directory

**Why it works:** Searches for "therapists near me" increased 49% from 2020-2023 and continue to climb. 1 in 4 US adults now seek mental health services. Psychology Today's therapist directory dominates but is ripe for niche disruption (e.g., by specialty, demographic, insurance accepted).

| Metric | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| Example search terms | "therapist near me", "find a therapist", "ADHD therapist", "Black female therapist near me" |
| Est. monthly US search volume | 500K-1M+ (combined related terms) |
| Monetization | Premium listings ($30-100/mo per therapist), lead gen, insurance verification tools |
| Competition level | Medium - dominated by Psychology Today, but niche angles wide open |
| Revenue potential | High - therapists pay $30-100/mo for directory listings |

**Niche angles:** Therapists by specialty (ADHD, trauma, couples), by demographic (LGBTQ+, BIPOC, Spanish-speaking), by modality (telehealth-only, EMDR, CBT), by insurance accepted.

## Key Takeaways

1. **AI Tools Directory is the #1 opportunity for 2026** - the market is exploding, competition is still fragmented, and monetization via affiliates is straightforward.
2. **Therapist and Daycare directories are underserved** relative to demand - both have emotional, high-intent searchers and limited quality options.
3. **Niche down aggressively** - specialized directories generate 340% higher revenue per listing than general ones. Don't build "another Yelp" -- build the definitive directory for a specific slice.
4. **Insurance/payment filtering is a killer feature** for healthcare directories (therapists, dentists, vets) -- it's the #1 pain point searchers have.
5. **The USA is the best market** for directory businesses due to high search volume, willingness to pay for premium listings, and advertising spend culture.
